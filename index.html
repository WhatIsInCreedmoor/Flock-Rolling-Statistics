How Flock Transparency Data Works

Flock Safety's public transparency portal publishes a rolling 30-day aggregate sum for vehicle detections, searches, and hotlist hits rather than individual daily breakdowns.

This dashboard runs an automated GitHub Action daily to capture snapshots of these rolling numbers. Over time, this builds a continuous historical record that reveals surveillance volume trends across Creedmoor.

About Creedmoor NC ALPRs

The Creedmoor Police Department maintains 10 Flock Safety automated license plate reader cameras placed along key transit corridors (US-15, NC-56, West Wilton Ave, and South Main St).

Plate data is retained for 30 days before scheduled deletion, and alerted against NCIC and Amber Alert hotlists.
